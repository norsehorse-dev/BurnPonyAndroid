BurnPony 2.0. New note types: Double bottom (one link, a separate compartment per passphrase) and Split key (opens only when enough key links are combined). Notes can be scheduled to open later. Open immediately skips the confirmation screen. Settings now holds default send options, read receipt included, and optional saved copies of what you send, locked by your phone's secure hardware. The Tor and proxy settings are now translated. Plus UI improvements.
